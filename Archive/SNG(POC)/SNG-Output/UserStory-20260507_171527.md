# User Story – Enable User Assignment to Duty Schedules

- **Application:** UeLS-zentras On-call System (Pikettsystem 2)
- **Source Document:** 6.3 Pikettsystem 2 en-US.pdf (v0.2)
- **Created:** May 7, 2026

---

## 1. Requirement Title

**Enable User Assignment to On-call Duty Schedules**

---

## 2. Business Objective

- **Business Need:** The Swiss Federal Roads Office (FEDRO) requires the ability to assign authenticated users (on-call participants) to on-call duty schedules so that infrastructure alarms received from UeLS-zentras are forwarded to the correct on-call personnel via SMS and pager notifications.
- **Value Delivered:** Ensures 24/7 on-call coverage for critical national road infrastructure by allowing planners to assign qualified personnel to specific duty periods and regions, with escalation paths when primary contacts are unreachable.
- **Why This Requirement Exists:** Without structured user-to-duty assignment, alarms from UeLS-zentras systems would have no designated recipient, resulting in unmonitored infrastructure alerts and potential safety risks on Swiss national roads.

---

## 3. Detailed Requirement Description

### Confirmed Requirements

1. **On-call Participant Registration:** An LDAP-authenticated user can be registered as an on-call participant by assigning one or more alarm media (cell phone or pager) to their login profile (Section 6.8).
2. **Alarm Media Assignment:** Each on-call participant can be linked to multiple alarm media. Each alarm medium has an identification (unique, not editable after creation), a number (numeric only), and a type (SMS or Pager) (Section 6.9).
3. **Active/Inactive Alarm Media:** Each assigned alarm medium can be individually activated or deactivated. If deactivated, alarms are not forwarded to that medium (Section 6.8).
4. **Period Assignment:** On-call participants are assigned to on-call periods within a basic plan. A basic plan is specific to a region and time interval (Section 5.2).
5. **Participant Selection During Period Operations:** When splitting, merging, or adjusting handover times of periods, the planner can select the on-call participant for the resulting period(s) (Sections 5.2.1, 5.2.2, 5.2.3).
6. **Warning on Mismatch:** When saving a period, if the assigned on-call participant differs from the originally planned participant, a warning message is displayed (Section 5.2.1).
7. **Info Box:** An info box shows the assigned participant details when a period is selected (Section 5.2).
8. **Escalation Assignment:** Each on-call period can optionally have an escalation participant. Each region can also have a region-level escalation participant. The escalation chain follows: regular participant → period escalation → region escalation (Section 2.2.10).
9. **Absence Awareness:** Absences (Vacation, Compensation, Sickness, Military/Civilian Service, Cleaning, Unpaid, Other) can be recorded per participant. Absences do not prevent assignment but trigger visual indicators during planning (Section 7.1).
10. **Holiday Warnings:** When an assigned period includes a public holiday, the system displays a warning to the planner (Section 7.2).
11. **SMS/Pager Forwarding:** When an alarm occurs and the participant is on duty in an active, non-paused region, SMS/pager messages are sent to all active alarm media of the assigned participant (Sections 1.4, 8.1).
12. **Evaluation Reports:** Assignment data is visible in overall planning, region, and participant evaluation reports. Conflicts are shown in red, multiple shifts in orange. Reports are exportable as CSV and XML (Chapter 9).

### Assumptions

1. The LDAP user must already exist in the directory before being assigned as an on-call participant.
2. At least one alarm medium must be active for an assigned participant to receive notifications.
3. The "planned participant" referenced in warning messages refers to a participant previously assigned during basic plan creation.

### Not Specified

- Maximum number of alarm media per participant is **not specified**.
- Whether a single user can be assigned to overlapping periods across different regions simultaneously is **not specified** as a restriction (though conflicts are shown in reports).
- Specific UI controls (dropdown, search field, etc.) for participant selection are **not specified** beyond the general configuration mask layout.
- Audit trail or logging of assignment changes is **not specified**.

## 4. User Story

**US-01: Register On-call Participant**

**As an** administrator  
**I want** to assign LDAP users to alarm media (cell phones and pagers) to register them as on-call participants  
**So that** they can receive alarm notifications when assigned to on-call duty

---

**US-02: Assign Participant to On-call Period**

**As an** on-call planner  
**I want** to assign a registered on-call participant to a specific on-call period within a basic plan  
**So that** alarms occurring during that period are forwarded to the responsible person

---

**US-03: Manage Alarm Media per Participant**

**As an** administrator  
**I want** to activate or deactivate individual alarm media for an on-call participant  
**So that** notifications are sent only to the participant's currently active devices

---

**US-04: Assign Participant During Period Split**

**As an** on-call planner  
**I want** to select on-call participants for both new periods when splitting an existing period  
**So that** each resulting sub-period has a designated on-call participant

---

**US-05: Assign Participant During Period Merge**

**As an** on-call planner  
**I want** to select the on-call participant for the merged period when combining two adjacent periods  
**So that** the resulting period has a clearly assigned responsible person

---

**US-06: Assign Participant During Handover Adjustment**

**As an** on-call planner  
**I want** to select on-call participants when adjusting the handover time between two periods  
**So that** participant assignments remain correct after the time boundary changes

---

**US-07: Configure Escalation Participant (Period Level)**

**As an** on-call planner  
**I want** to assign an escalation on-call participant for a specific period  
**So that** if the regular participant is unreachable, alarms are forwarded to the backup person

---

**US-08: Configure Escalation Participant (Region Level)**

**As an** administrator  
**I want** to assign a region-level escalation on-call participant  
**So that** there is a final fallback when both the regular and period-level escalation participants are unreachable

---

**US-09: View Assignment Conflicts in Reports**

**As a** user  
**I want** to view evaluation reports showing participant assignments with conflict highlighting (red) and multiple-shift indicators (orange)  
**So that** I can identify and resolve scheduling issues

---

**US-10: Receive Warning for Absent Participant Assignment**

**As an** on-call planner  
**I want** the system to display a warning when I assign a participant to a period during which they have a recorded absence  
**So that** I am aware of potential availability issues before finalizing the schedule

---

## 5. Functional Scope

- Register LDAP users as on-call participants by assigning alarm media
- Create, edit, and manage alarm media (cell phone, pager) with identification, number, and type
- Activate/deactivate individual alarm media per participant
- Assign on-call participants to on-call periods within basic plans
- Select participants during period split, merge, and handover adjustment operations
- Display warning when assigned participant differs from originally planned participant
- Display participant details in info box on period selection
- Assign escalation participants at period level and region level
- Escalation chain execution: regular → period escalation → region escalation
- Record participant absences (7 types) with visual indicators during planning
- Display holiday warnings when assigned periods overlap with public holidays
- Forward SMS/pager messages to all active alarm media of the assigned participant when alarms occur
- Generate evaluation reports (overall plan, region, participant) showing assignments, conflicts, and absences
- Export evaluation reports as CSV and XML

---

## 6. Out of Scope

- LDAP user creation and management (handled externally)
- SSO/LDAP authentication infrastructure
- F24 eCall backend message delivery service (third-party)
- On-call region creation and configuration (separate feature)
- Basic plan creation and period structure management (separate feature)
- Auto-pause, alarm delay, and shipping delay configuration (separate features)
- System activation and group-to-region assignment (separate features)
- UeLS-zentras data point and alarm management (separate application)
- Network failover between Stans and Senti cluster nodes

---

## 7. Preconditions

- UeLS-zentras application must be running and accessible
- LDAP service must be available for user authentication
- The user performing the assignment must have sufficient authorization (administrator or on-call planner role)
- At least one on-call region must be configured and active
- At least one on-call basic plan with defined periods must exist for the target region
- Alarm media (cell phones/pagers) must be created before they can be assigned to participants
- F24 eCall interface must be accessible for SMS/pager delivery (for end-to-end alarm forwarding)

---

## 8. Trigger

| Trigger | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| User Action | Administrator navigates to Configuration View → On-call Participants tab to register a participant |
| User Action | On-call planner selects a period in the Planning View and assigns/changes the on-call participant |
| User Action | On-call planner initiates a split, merge, or handover adjustment and selects participants for the resulting periods |
| System Event | Alarm received from UeLS-zentras triggers SMS/pager forwarding to the assigned on-call participant |
| System Event | eCall delivery failure triggers escalation to the next participant in the chain |

## 9. Main Success Flow

### Flow A: Register On-call Participant

1. Administrator navigates to **Configuration View → On-call Participants**
2. Administrator selects an alarm medium from the available alarm media list
3. Administrator enters the **Login** (LDAP username) of the user to be configured as on-call participant
4. System displays the alarm medium's **Number** and **Type** (read-only)
5. Administrator sets the **Active** flag to **Yes** for this alarm medium assignment
6. Administrator optionally adds additional alarm media for the same participant
7. Administrator clicks **Save**
8. System validates the input and saves the on-call participant configuration
9. The participant is now available for assignment to on-call periods

### Flow B: Assign Participant to Period

1. On-call planner navigates to **Planning View → Period Planning**
2. Planner selects a region and views the on-call periods within the basic plan
3. Planner selects an on-call period to edit
4. Planner selects the **on-call participant** from the participant list
5. Planner optionally selects an **escalation participant** for the period
6. Planner clicks **Save**
7. System checks if the selected participant matches the originally planned participant
8. If different, system displays a **warning message**
9. Planner acknowledges the warning (if any) and confirms the save
10. System saves the assignment; the participant is now scheduled for on-call duty during that period

### Flow C: Alarm Forwarding to Assigned Participant

1. UeLS-zentras sends an alarm to the on-call system
2. System identifies the region for the alarm based on system/group assignment
3. System identifies the current on-call period for the region
4. System retrieves the assigned on-call participant for the active period
5. System sends SMS/pager messages to all **active** alarm media of the assigned participant via F24 eCall
6. System records the SMS/pager message with delivery status (Pending → Sending → Sent/Error)
7. If delivery fails (based on eCall response), system escalates to the **period-level escalation participant**
8. If period-level escalation also fails, system escalates to the **region-level escalation participant**

## 10. Alternate Flows

### AF-01: No Alarm Media Assigned
- **Trigger:** Administrator attempts to save a participant without any alarm media
- **Expected:** System displays a plausibility error (⚠) next to the input field; save is prevented
- **Source:** Section 6.8

### AF-02: All Alarm Media Deactivated
- **Trigger:** All alarm media for the assigned participant are set to inactive
- **Expected:** Alarms are not forwarded to the participant's devices; escalation may be triggered
- **Source:** Section 6.8

### AF-03: Participant Differs from Planned
- **Trigger:** Planner saves a period with a different participant than originally planned
- **Expected:** System displays a warning message; planner can still proceed with save
- **Source:** Section 5.2.1

### AF-04: Participant Has Recorded Absence
- **Trigger:** Participant assigned to a period has an overlapping absence (vacation, sickness, etc.)
- **Expected:** System displays a visual indicator (absence shown in cell); assignment is not blocked
- **Source:** Section 7.1

### AF-05: Period Includes Public Holiday
- **Trigger:** Assigned period overlaps with a configured public holiday
- **Expected:** System displays a warning message to the planner
- **Source:** Section 7.2

### AF-06: Region Is Paused
- **Trigger:** Alarm occurs for a region that is currently paused
- **Expected:** No SMS/pager messages are sent to the assigned participant; no entry is created in the SMS/pager list
- **Source:** Section 6.2

### AF-07: System Is Inactive
- **Trigger:** Alarm occurs for an inactive system
- **Expected:** Alarm is not forwarded to the on-call participant
- **Source:** Section 6.7

### AF-08: eCall Delivery Failure (Invalid Phone Number)
- **Trigger:** F24 eCall returns error 5112 (invalid phone number) for the assigned participant's alarm medium
- **Expected:** System records the error; attempts escalation to next participant in the chain
- **Source:** Section 8.4

### AF-09: eCall Timeout
- **Trigger:** F24 eCall returns error 1001 (timeout)
- **Expected:** System records the failure; retransmission attempts are made per eCall configuration
- **Source:** Section 8.4

### AF-10: Insufficient Authorization
- **Trigger:** User without administrator/planner rights attempts to assign participants
- **Expected:** Functionality is not visible or accessible due to authorization restrictions
- **Source:** Section 1.3, Section 2.1

### AF-11: Duplicate Message During Failover
- **Trigger:** Communication interruption between Stans and Senti cluster nodes
- **Expected:** Assigned participant may receive duplicate alarm messages; manual deactivation of regions at one location is the workaround
- **Source:** Section 2 – Application architecture

### AF-12: User Cancellation
- **Trigger:** Planner clicks **Reset** instead of **Save** after modifying participant assignment
- **Expected:** Input fields revert to the previously saved values; no changes are persisted
- **Source:** Section 10 – System configuration view (general button behavior)

---

## 11. Assumptions

1. LDAP users are pre-existing in the directory; the on-call system does not create LDAP accounts.
2. The "originally planned participant" in warning messages refers to the participant previously saved for that period, not a system-suggested default.
3. An on-call participant must have at least one active alarm medium to effectively receive alarm notifications.
4. The escalation chain is processed sequentially (regular → period → region) and does not loop or retry the regular participant.
5. Absence recording is informational and advisory; it does not enforce scheduling restrictions.
6. The participant selection interface is available consistently across all period operations (create, split, merge, handover adjustment).
7. Authorization to assign participants requires the same privilege level as editing on-call periods.
