# Lump Sum Investment Strategy Guide

**For:** Investors with existing SIP + occasional surplus funds  
**Context:** You have a running SIP of ₹20,000/month and occasionally receive lump sum amounts (₹20K–₹50K+) from savings, bonuses, or reduced expenses.

---

## Understanding Lump Sum Investment Options

| Option | What It Means | Best When |
|--------|---------------|-----------|
| **Direct Lump Sum** | Invest entire amount at once | Markets are down/corrected |
| **Systematic Transfer Plan (STP)** | Park in liquid fund, transfer gradually | Markets at all-time high |
| **Top-up Existing Funds** | Add to your current 8 funds | Regular surplus (monthly) |
| **Opportunistic Buying** | Buy specific fund during dip | Sector/category correction |

## Strategy 3: Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) Method

### What is STP?

| Step | Action | Purpose |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Invest lump sum in Liquid Fund | Park money safely (5-6% returns) |
| 2 | Set up weekly/monthly transfer | Move to equity funds gradually |
| 3 | Transfers happen automatically | Rupee-cost averaging benefit |

### STP Setup Example (₹50,000)

| Week | Transfer Amount | From | To | Remaining in Liquid |
|------|-----------------|------|-----|---------------------|
| 1 | ₹12,500 | Liquid Fund | Parag Parikh Flexi Cap | ₹37,500 |
| 2 | ₹12,500 | Liquid Fund | Mirae Asset Large & Midcap | ₹25,000 |
| 3 | ₹12,500 | Liquid Fund | Kotak Emerging Equity | ₹12,500 |
| 4 | ₹12,500 | Liquid Fund | Nippon India Small Cap | ₹0 |

### Recommended Liquid Funds for Parking

| Fund Name | Expense Ratio | 1-Year Return | Exit Load |
|-----------|---------------|---------------|-----------|
| Parag Parikh Liquid Fund - Direct | 0.20% | 6.2% | Nil after 7 days |
| HDFC Liquid Fund - Direct | 0.20% | 6.1% | Nil after 7 days |
| ICICI Prudential Liquid Fund - Direct | 0.20% | 6.0% | Nil after 7 days |

## Strategy 4: Opportunistic Buying During Corrections

### Category Correction Triggers

| Scenario | Opportunity | Action |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Small caps down 15%+ (while large caps stable) | Small Cap on sale | Allocate 50%+ to small cap funds |
| Mid caps down 10%+ | Mid Cap value | Allocate 40%+ to mid cap funds |
| Overall market down 20%+ | Everything on sale | Invest immediately across all funds |
| Specific sector crash (IT, Banking) | Sector opportunity | Consider sectoral/thematic fund (optional) |

### Quick Correction Response Table

| Lump Sum Amount | Market Down 10% | Market Down 20% | Market Down 30%+ |
|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|------------------|
| ₹20,000 | Invest in Flexi Cap | Split: Flexi + Mid Cap | All in Mid/Small Cap |
| ₹50,000 | Follow normal allocation | Increase Mid/Small % | Heavy Mid/Small tilt |
| ₹1,00,000 | STP over 1 month | Invest 70% immediately | Invest 100% immediately |

## Tax Considerations for Lump Sum

| Holding Period | Tax Type | Tax Rate | Exemption |
|----------------|----------|----------|-----------|
| Less than 1 year | STCG | 20% | None |
| More than 1 year | LTCG | 12.5% | ₹1.25 Lakh/year |

### Tax Planning Tip

| If You Have | Consider |
|-------------|----------|
| Multiple lump sum investments in same year | Track purchase dates |
| Approaching 1-year holding | Wait to redeem after 1 year |
| Gains approaching ₹1.25 Lakh | Book gains before year-end to use exemption |

## Summary: Quick Decision Guide

| Lump Sum Amount | Market Normal | Market Down 15%+ |
|-----------------|---------------|------------------|
| ₹10K–₹25K | Add to top 2 funds (60:40) | All to undervalued category |
| ₹25K–₹50K | 2-week STP or direct split | Invest directly in 3-4 funds |
| ₹50K–₹1L | 4-week STP recommended | Invest 70% now, 30% in 2 weeks |
| ₹1L+ | Use STP over 8-12 weeks | Invest 60% now, rest via STP |

---

## Key Takeaways

| Principle | Explanation |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Don't let money sit idle** | Even savings account earns less than inflation |
| **STP reduces timing risk** | Protects against investing at market peak |
| **Market corrections = opportunities** | Lump sum works best when markets are down |
| **Stay consistent** | Don't skip SIP because you invested lump sum |
| **Keep it simple** | When confused, just add to Flexi Cap fund |

---

*This strategy complements your existing SIP plan. Lump sum investments should enhance, not replace, your regular SIP discipline.*
