
I will provide you with a short one-line requirement.Expand it into a complete, detailed, structured, and testable requirement/user story that can be used by development and testing teams.Please analyse the requirement carefully and generate the output in the following structure:
1. Requirement Title: Create a clear and meaningful title based on the one-line requirement.
2. Business Objective: Explain the business need or purpose behind this requirement. Mention what problem it solves or what value it provides.
3. Detailed Requirement Description: Expand the one-line requirement into a detailed functional description. Explain what the system should do, when it should happen, who can perform the action, what inputs are required, what outputs are expected, and how the user/system flow should behave.
4. User Story:Write the requirement in Agile user story format:
As a [type of user],I want [some goal/functionality],So that [business benefit/value].
5. Functional Scope:Clearly mention what is included in the scope of this requirement.
6. Out of Scope: Mention what is not covered or should not be assumed as part of this requirement.
7. Preconditions: List the conditions that must exist before this functionality can be used.
8. Trigger: Mention what action or event starts this functionality.
9. Main Success Flow: Provide a step-by-step flow explaining how the functionality should work in the ideal successful scenario.1
10. Alternate Flows:List possible alternate paths or variations in the workflow.
11. Assumptions: Clearly list all assumptions made while expanding the one-line requirement.

 

Important Instructions:- 
Do not hallucinate final business rules.- Clearly separate confirmed details from assumptions.
Keep the language clear, professional, and implementation-friendly.
