# To improve

## Scope Change Communication and Effort Tracking

If the scope of a user story increases, including any UI changes beyond the initially agreed requirements, it should be communicated immediately. Additional effort for such changes should be assessed and tracked through a task to avoid impacting the planned scope and timelines. Consistently recording the actual effort spent on each story was also highlighted, as it supports more accurate estimation and provides greater transparency into the team's delivery and capacity.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**

## Copado Knowledge Transfer and Enablement

Knowledge transfer sessions on Copado should be planned so that all team members are well aware of its new features. Additionally, Account Engagement and Copado KT sessions should be scheduled to strengthen the team's overall platform knowledge and reduce dependency on individual expertise.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**

## Independent Deployment Access

Team members expressed the need for Copado deployment access to independently perform deployments. Providing this access will reduce dependency on others and improve deployment efficiency and team autonomy.

**– 1 Members contributed this point**

## Code Quality and Modern Salesforce Standards

Code quality should be improved by modifying the existing codebase in line with the latest Salesforce standards, ensuring the solutions remain scalable, maintainable, and aligned with platform best practices.

**– 1 Members contributed this point**

## Improving the Way of Working

The team's overall way of working can be further improved, reflecting an ongoing commitment to refining processes and collaboration practices.
