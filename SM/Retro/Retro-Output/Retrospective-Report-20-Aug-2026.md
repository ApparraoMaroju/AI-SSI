# To improve

## Code Quality and Modern Salesforce Practices

The team should place greater focus on improving code quality and adopting Salesforce-recommended approaches to ensure solutions remain scalable, reliable, and easier to maintain. Exploring Agentforce capabilities for potential use within the project was also suggested as a forward-looking improvement area.

**– 1 Members contributed this point**

## Recognition of Testing Contributions Alongside Development

When complex user stories are completed and deployed without major defects, the achievement is often attributed primarily to development effort. The significant and challenging work involved in thoroughly testing and validating these stories should be equally recognized, as this will encourage stronger collaboration, improve team morale, and reinforce the importance of quality throughout the delivery process.

**– 1 Members contributed this point**

## Reducing Manual Post-Deployment Effort

The team identified an opportunity to reduce manual post-deployment steps, such as translations and field permission updates, by making greater use of Copado's newer capabilities. Paying closer attention to post-deployment activities overall was also highlighted as an area for improvement to reduce risk and manual overhead.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**

## Greater Attention to Detail to Prevent Production Defects

Some production issues were traced back to minor details being missed. Improving attention to small details during implementation and review will help reduce the recurrence of such defects and strengthen overall delivery quality.
