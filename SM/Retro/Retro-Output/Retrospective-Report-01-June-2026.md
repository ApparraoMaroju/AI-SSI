# To improve

## Meeting scheduling discipline

The team highlighted the need to keep meeting dates and times stable once planned, as last-minute changes can disrupt daily planning and reduce predictability. Better schedule discipline will help the team organize work more effectively and avoid unnecessary rescheduling overhead.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**

## Stronger requirement clarity in refinement

Several comments pointed to the need for clearer requirements and acceptance criteria at the start of each user story, along with more active questioning during refinement. Improving early clarification will reduce rework, shorten acceptance cycles, and increase delivery confidence.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**

## Improve status hygiene and time tracking

Consistent logging of actual time against stories, along with timely ticket and sub-task status updates, was identified as an area for improvement. Stronger execution hygiene will improve visibility for the wider team and make progress tracking more reliable.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**

## Broader knowledge sharing and meeting engagement

The team recommended more cross-functional knowledge sharing so that delivery context is better understood across all members, especially when work is executed independently. Keeping cameras on during meetings was also suggested to improve engagement and collaboration during discussions.

**– 2 Members contributed this point**
