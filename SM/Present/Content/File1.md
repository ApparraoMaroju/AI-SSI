# Requirement Analysis Process

## Objective

Ensure every Product Backlog Item (PBI) is fully understood, designed, estimated, and validated before entering a Sprint.

## Inputs

- Business Requests
- User Feedback
- Product Owner Priorities
- Operational Enhancements
- Compliance Requirements

## Output

- Ready for Sprint User Stories

---

# Backlog Refinement

## Purpose

Refine Product Backlog Items until they satisfy the Definition of Ready (DoR).

## Activities

1. Product Owner selects User Stories.
2. Stories are prioritized for refinement.
3. Team reviews business context.
4. Functional discussion.
5. Technical discussion.
6. Dependency identification.
7. Initial solution proposal.
8. Story estimation.
9. DoR validation.
10. Move to **Ready for Sprint**.

# Definition of Ready (DoR)

## Quality Gate

A User Story can enter Sprint Planning only after satisfying all DoR criteria.

### Checklist

- Business value defined
- Acceptance criteria available
- Initial solution completed
- Dependencies identified
- Story estimated
- INVEST validated

---

# INVEST Criteria

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Independent | Can be implemented independently |
| Negotiable | Details can evolve through discussion |
| Valuable | Delivers business value |
| Estimable | Team can estimate effort |
| Small | Fits within a Sprint |
| Testable | Acceptance criteria are measurable |
